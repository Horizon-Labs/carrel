Carrel 1.0.0-rc.1 is the first release candidate for 1.0. One archive now works across your Mac, iPad and iPhone: it lives in iCloud Drive as the same plain files, one device at a time writes its history, and every other device still acts, even offline, through its own inbox. The iPad and iPhone apps are full apps built from the Mac app's code, not viewers. The Mac reaches further into macOS, with Spotlight, Quick Look, Finder, the share sheet and Shortcuts. And you can send feedback from any screen, tied to the screen and step you were on. Everything still stays on your devices.

This is a release candidate for testing. Fixes go into the next candidates, and 1.0.0 follows after two weeks of daily use on a candidate with no blocking issues (#38). The planned v0.7 and v0.8 releases ship together here instead.

## Feedback from any screen

- **Where:** Help → Send Feedback in the Mac app, a shake or the ••• menu on iPad and iPhone, `!` in the interactive mode, and `carrel feedback "text"` on the command line.
- **What it records:** the screen and step you were on, the items involved, Carrel's version and commit, the device and model, recent errors, and an optional screenshot.
- **Where it goes:** into the archive, under `_System/feedback/`, one folder per device. `carrel feedback list` and `carrel feedback export` gather it, and `carrel feedback issue N` opens a prefilled GitHub issue for you to submit. Nothing is posted for you.

## iCloud Drive and more than one device

- **The archive in iCloud Drive,** as plain files, readable in Finder, Files and Obsidian. `carrel sync move` moves an archive there, checking every file of the copy, and `carrel sync status` shows the writer, the inbox backlog and what's waiting.
- **One writer at a time:** a CloudKit lock records which device writes the history, until when, and the history it last committed. It holds no file contents. On a Mac, the background agent holds it for the app and the command line.
- **Inboxes for every other device:** every operation can be sent through a device's own inbox, and the next writer applies them in order, each recorded in History as that device's. An entry that no longer applies is recorded as skipped, with the reason.
- **Files iCloud has evicted** are downloaded when Carrel needs them, never treated as missing. A collection can be kept downloaded on a device.
- **The holding folder:** on devices without Kopia, content that's replaced or removed is kept until a Mac snapshots it. Status says how much is waiting, and held versions can be restored.
- **Capability profiles:** each device records what it can do. A card drafted below the preferred level goes to a re-check queue for a stronger device.

## iPad and iPhone

- **One app for iPad and iPhone,** built from the Mac app's code: review, questions, browse, search, the librarian, discovery, checkouts, the desk, history and settings.
- **Drafting on the device** with Apple's model or an MLX model, chosen by the device itself.
- **Apple Pencil notes on cards,** handwritten and recognized, shown in every interface.
- **Spotlight** finds your cards, and **reminders** arrive as notifications.

## macOS

- **Spotlight** indexes cards from the agent (`spotlight.index`).
- **Quick Look** previews cards and Carrel's drawings.
- **Finder:** badges, and Check Out, Return, Show in Carrel and Ask About This in its context menu.
- **Add to Archive** from Finder's Quick Actions, the Services menu and the share sheet, for files, web addresses and notes. A web address or a note can be added from Carrel's own window too, in every interface.
- **Shortcuts, Siri and Apple Intelligence:** App Intents to add, search, ask, check out, return and take a snapshot.

## The librarian and drafting

- **The librarian knows what you're looking at,** in every interface.
- **Flags:** flag a collection or a card that needs work while browsing, and the librarian looks, fixes it, asks for details, or says why it can't.
- **Two or three readings** of what an item is, when the model isn't sure.
- **Redrafting:** cards are redrafted when a better model is installed or Carrel's drafting improves.
- **Restructure proposals** now come from real embeddings, can move items into existing collections, and use the crawler's verdicts.
- **Missing functionality** can be reported from a local gap log.
- **Opening at a screen:** the interactive mode and the Mac app open at a given screen.

## Not in this candidate

- **Signed and notarized builds** (#47): the Mac app and the iPad and iPhone apps are built with Xcode and installed directly for now.
- **The user guides and gate checklists** for macOS integration (#413) and iCloud, iPad and iPhone (#473).
- **Handwritten notes** can't yet be sent through the inbox from a device that isn't the writer (#497), or removed in the interactive mode (#495).
- **Checked by hand:** much of this release was built and tested on simulators, but not yet on the real devices. This candidate is for that.

## Install

- **The command line:** download the archive for your platform below, unpack it, and put its programs together in a folder on your `PATH`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and each archive has a build attestation (`gh attestation verify FILE --repo Horizon-Labs/carrel`).
- **The Mac app, iPad and iPhone:** for now, build them with Xcode and install them directly, as [The Mac app](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/Mac-App) explains. TestFlight comes once the App Store Connect record is set up.
- **iCloud:** sign in to iCloud on each device, then move the archive into iCloud Drive with `carrel sync move`.

The [user guide](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/User-Guide) explains each part, and [Command-Line Tool](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/Command-Line-Tool) lists every command.
